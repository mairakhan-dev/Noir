Our story

Built for slow dinners and unforgettable evenings.

NOIR began with an idea: serving a room that feels like an intimate stage set—moody, warm, and carefully orchestrated. Our kitchen works with the seasons, sourcing exceptional ingredients from trusted growers and purveyors who share our obsession with craft.

We believe the best dining experience is not an act, but a rhythm: a well-timed first pour, a gentle hush in the room, plates arriving with intention, and a final course that stays with you long after the last sip.

N
Nicolas Vale
Founder & Chef
